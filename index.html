Drag a piece onto the grid, or tap once to carry it and tap again to drop. R rotates while carrying · Esc cancels · U undoes · N deals a new puzzle.

The categories

Any picture from a set counts as that category. A row, column or box may hold each category only once.

Options

Grey squares are clues — you may park a piece on top of one, but a buried clue always strands an empty square, so no finished grid has one.
