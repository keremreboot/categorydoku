Drag a piece onto the grid, or tap once to carry it and tap again to drop. R rotates while carrying · Esc cancels · U undoes · N deals a new puzzle.

The categories

Each box on the board belongs to one of these categories and holds a different picture from it in every square. Pieces are cut across box edges, so one piece can carry two or three categories at once — the mix, and how it is arranged, is what tells you where it goes.

Options

Grey squares are clues — they show you what a box's category is. You may park a piece on top of one, but a buried clue always strands an empty square, so no finished grid has one.
